Nothing from SlaveTatsNG, SlaveTats, JContainers or SKSE Menu Framework is redistributed here; all four are separate dependencies you install yourself. The DLL does contain compiled-in code from the libraries above, which is why their license texts are included, and [code]THIRD_PARTY.md[/code] in the download names the exact revisions plus the two vendored headers that carry local changes.
